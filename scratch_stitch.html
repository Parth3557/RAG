Backpropagation: The Art of Learning from Mistakes

Imagine you're practicing archery blindfolded. You shoot an arrow, and a coach tells you how far you missed the bullseye. Backpropagation is essentially that coach calculating exactly how much to adjust your arm's angle and strength for the next shot.

forward The Forward Pass

The data flows through the network. Each layer makes a 'guess' based on its current weights. It ends with a final prediction.

settings_backup_restore The Backward Pass

We calculate the Error (Loss). Then, we work backward from the output to the input, figuring out which neurons contributed most to the error.
